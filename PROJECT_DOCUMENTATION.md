1. Project Overview

HANGA-AKAZI is a proposed digital platform designed to help young people in Rwanda discover job opportunities, training programmes, internships, and entrepreneurship opportunities.

The platform aims to use labour-market statistics from the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) and connect users to trusted opportunity sources.

2. Problem Statement

Young people may struggle to find reliable information about employment and training opportunities. Information is often spread across different websites, and users may have difficulty identifying opportunities that match their skills and qualifications.

3. Project Objectives

- Make employment and training information easier to find.
- Connect users to trusted opportunity sources.
- Use official statistics to explain labour-market challenges.
- Help users identify opportunities relevant to their profiles.
- Develop a mobile-friendly platform.

4. Target Users

- Young job seekers.
- Students and recent graduates.
- People seeking training and internships.
- Aspiring entrepreneurs.
- Employers and training providers.

5. Current Prototype Features

- Responsive website interface.
- Labour-market statistics dashboard.
- User profile form.
- Opportunity search and filtering.
- Opportunity matching scores.
- Saved opportunities.
- Links to external opportunity and statistics sources.

Important: Test each feature before marking it as completed. Browser-based storage is not a shared database.

8. Data Verification Policy

Statistics must be checked against the original NISR report before publication.

Every opportunity should include its source and verification status. A demonstration listing must never be presented as a confirmed vacancy.

NISR is a source of statistical information; it should not be described as an employer or job portal.

9. Technical Limitations

The current GitHub Pages prototype is a static website. Data stored in browser "localStorage" is generally limited to that browser and device. It does not automatically synchronise information across users.

A shared database and secure administration features would require additional development.
